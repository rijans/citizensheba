# Partner listing (associations-first, docs-only)

Citizens look for industry and trade associations (e.g. BASIS) that are useful but are **not** government / utility / half-gov Outbounds. Stuffing them into Official Services would break catalog trust; inventing an ad-hoc “non-Official” hop without rules would invite doorway spam. We lock **Partner listing** as a distinct type: a curated outbound hop to a non-gov org we willingly list, **never** labeled Official. **Allowlist v1** is membership-based industry / trade / civic associations with a public useful portal. **Explicitly out of Partner for this slice:** private banks, MFS apps, commercial SaaS, scrapers, National Portal mirrors, and **Promoted** / **Affiliate** (still future separate types). **Surface today:** decision and glossary only — no published Partner hops, no schema field, no Instant Directory / `directory-index.json` / Category Official-list inclusion. When Partner hops ship later, they must use distinct Non-Official page chrome, one Outbound, thin-hop discipline (no doorway spam), and must not reuse Official SERP/trust copy; Partners must not enter Instant Directory discovery until a **follow-up ADR** amends that gate. Related Partner ↔ Official links are allowed only after Partner hops exist and must not imply the Partner is Official. We rejected an umbrella NonOfficial type, stuffing associations into Official, and shipping Partner UI before this ADR.
