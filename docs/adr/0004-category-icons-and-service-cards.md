# Category Icons and shared Service cards

Instant Directory cards were text-only and hard to scan; category content already stored unused lucide-style `icon` keys. We ship **Lucide** Category Icons with **soft accents mapped in code** by category id, reuse the same Service card on Home, Category Pages, and related Services (icon, title/title_bn, description, official domain, status when not ACTIVE), and keep chips as small icon + EN name. **Optional Service `icon`** (Lucide key) overrides the glyph on that Service’s cards while the soft accent stays Category-colored; omit `icon` to keep the Category glyph. Chips always use Category icons. We rejected emoji-first cards (less civic trust), YAML-edited accent colors (inconsistency), per-Service accent colors, custom illustrated stock ID/passport PNGs, and a Home trust strip (footer Non-Official Disclaimer is enough).
